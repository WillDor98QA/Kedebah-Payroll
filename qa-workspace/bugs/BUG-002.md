## Expected (per PRD §10/§24)
"Tier 1 (SSNIT) … 5.5% employee / **13% employer** of basic." For basic GH₵3,000 → employer GH₵390.

## Actual (live)
Tier 1 employer = **8%** (GH₵240 on basic 3,000). Tier 2 employer = 5% (GH₵150).
Total employer pension = 13% of basic (240 + 150).

## Analysis
The employee rate (5.5%) matches the PRD. Only the **employer split** differs. The live figures
match Ghana's actual scheme (Tier 1 = 13.5% total: 5.5% EE + 8% ER; Tier 2 = 5% ER), so the **app is
most likely correct** and **PRD §24 likely conflates** the 13% *total* employer rate with the Tier 1
employer rate. Impact: employer cost & statutory-remittance figures computed by the engine are 5% of
basic lower than a literal reading of the PRD would expect (per employee, per period).

## Evidence
- `evidence/network/run-80-employees-full.json` (Tier 1 line: amount 165 / employer_amount 240 on basic 3,000)
- Recorded FAIL: `test-management/test-execution-report.md` (feature "Tier 1 employer rate")
- Spec: `automation/tests/payroll/calc-coverage.live.spec.ts` (expected-to-fail test)

## Suggested Resolution
Confirm the authoritative Ghana Tier 1 employer rate. If 8% is correct, **fix PRD §10/§24** to read
"Tier 1: 5.5% employee / 8% employer; Tier 2: 0% / 5% (13% total employer)". If 13% is intended,
fix the statutory seed. Until dispositioned, the oracle's `GHANA_TIER1.employer` (0.13) stays aligned
to the PRD so the discrepancy keeps surfacing.
